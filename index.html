What are EM waves?

A simple way to understand them

Energy in motion ⚡

Electromagnetic waves are waves made from changing electric and magnetic fields. Unlike mechanical waves, they can travel through empty space, so sunlight can reach Earth from the Sun.

c = fλ

speed = frequency × wavelength

Why are they useful?

Different electromagnetic waves have different frequencies and wavelengths. This gives them different properties and makes them useful for communication, heating, sensing, seeing, imaging and medical treatment.

Applications in Real Life

Where do we use electromagnetic waves?

📻

Radio Waves

Used for radio and television broadcasting, wireless communication and some navigation systems.

COMMUNICATION
📡

Microwaves

Used in radar, satellite communication, mobile networks and microwave ovens.

RADAR • COOKING
🔥

Infrared

Used in remote controls, thermal cameras, heat sensors and some communication systems.

HEAT • SENSING
💡

Visible Light

The part of the spectrum detected by our eyes. Used in lighting, cameras, displays and optical communication.

VISION • LIGHT
☀️

Ultraviolet

Used for sterilization and detecting some substances. Too much UV exposure can damage skin and eyes.

STERILIZATION
🩻

X-Rays

Used mainly for medical imaging because X-rays can pass through soft tissue more easily than bone.

MEDICAL IMAGING
☢️

Gamma Rays

High-energy electromagnetic radiation used in some medical treatments and sterilization applications.

MEDICINE
